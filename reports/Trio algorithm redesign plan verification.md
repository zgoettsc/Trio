## 2. New or remaining internal contradictions in revision 2

1. **TBR <54 margin.** Conventions pre-register "TBR <54 **+0.3 pp**" as a CI margin; §4 says "TBR <54 is too rare for a CI at this scale; it is governed by the event-count rule". Delete it from the CI list or qualify it.
2. **28 paired days vs ABAB with washout.** §4 requires 28 paired days per arm from "two 28-day cycles", but the alternating-week ABAB with a 24-h washout after each switch leaves ≈6 analysable days per week → ≈24 per arm. Either three cycles or drop the washout from the count.
3. **Arm lengths per step.** S2.4 "Impact. 1 cycle" and S2.1/S2.3 "biweekly ABAB, 2 cycles" do not obviously meet the 28-paired-days minimum; S1.1/S1.3/S1.4 say "2 × 28-day cycles". Use one phrasing everywhere.
4. **Hard floor vs kill switch.** §3.8 declares the hard floor/ceiling "`supervisorHardFloorEnabled`, default true, immune to `killSwitch`", but the kill-switch convention says the pipeline then "runs exactly two things" and lands on "core only" before the supervisor was ever promoted. A default-true floor also doses differently while the supervisor is still `shadow` (Stage 0/1 "nothing doses differently"). State whether 3.8 is a sub-rule of the supervisor stage (then it is off until promotion) or a separate always-on stage (then list it in the kill-switch landing state and in S1.1's shadow gate).
5. **§3.3 formula claim.** Text says the formula yields 0.3 for (basal 1.0, core 0.3, v −0.5); the formula yields 1.0 and the constructor clamps to 0.3 (item 1).
6. **S1.4(a) `cgmQuality != unknown or …`** is vacuous and admits `suspect`/`stale` (item 3).
7. **Acceleration fallback** missing for §3.3, S1.4(b)(c), S2.1 (item 2).
8. **S0.8 keeps the signal export** vs S0.0/stop condition 12 (item 8).
9. **Timeline dev-day load.** Week 1 carries 7 dev-days (S0.0 0.5 + S0.1 0.5 + S0.2 6) and week 6 carries 9 (S0.6 5 + S0.8 4) in 5-day weeks; S0.0 and S0.8 were inserted without re-spreading. Stage 0 total (46) and the critical path (S0.2 → S0.3 → S0.4 → S0.8 → S1.1) are consistent; prerequisites of S1.1 (S0.8) and S1.3 (S0.7, S0.8) are satisfied by the week table.
10. **Minor wording.** Kill switch "removes all derived profile/preference overrides" although the conventions say nothing is ever patched in `preferences`; harmless.
