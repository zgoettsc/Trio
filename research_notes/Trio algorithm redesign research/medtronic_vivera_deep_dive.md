## Key Question 1 — Every quantitative result ever disclosed for Vivera/NMX-AID, by mode

### Takeaway
Only two small feasibility cohorts exist in public: (a) an adult cohort (n=14, Australia/St Vincent's Melbourne-type design) for which the company reports **73.8% TIR "without manual user input"** and ~**82% with announced meals**; and (b) a New Zealand cohort presented by B. Wheeler at ATTD 2026 (n=12 analysed, described in community coverage as "13 children") whose per-phase slide shows **64.7% TIR in the fully unannounced phase** (TBR <70 mg/dL 1.9%, <54 mg/dL 0.4%, mean SG 161.6 mg/dL) versus 67.8% on 780G with announcements and 73.7% on NMX7 with announcements. No TBR/TAR has ever been disclosed for the adult cohort, no peer-reviewed paper or accessible abstract text exists, and no pivotal (GATEWAY) data have been released.

### Inferences
- The two cohorts are most plausibly: adults (n=14, registered target 15, St Vincent's Melbourne, PI O'Neal) → 73.8%/74% unannounced and ~82% announced; and the NZ Wheeler cohort (n=12 analysed, "13 children" in community retelling) → 64.7% unannounced. The slide's 73.7% is the NMX7-*announced* arm of the Wheeler cohort and is a coincidental near-match to the company's 73.8% "no input" adult figure — do not conflate them.
- In the one per-arm table available, removing announcements cost ~9 TIR points vs NMX7-announced (73.7→64.7) and ~3 points vs the participants' own 780G baseline (67.8→64.7), with no TBR penalty (1.9% vs 1.9%) and the cost showing up as TAR (24.1→32.9%) and mean SG (+13 mg/dL). "At-will" bolusing (1.1 announcements/day, 40 g/day announced) recovered roughly a third of the gap (67.9%).
- TDD did not rise materially when announcements were removed (45.5→46.7 U/d), i.e., the unannounced mode did not simply deliver more insulin; it delivered it later.
- The "two three-week periods" (ADA poster description) vs "30 days + 30 days, 24 persons" (MedTech Dive 4 Jun) vs "three months" (Diabetech) are not contradictory if the ADA poster pooled only the announced/unannounced stages of a ~12–16-week 4-stage protocol; but the n=24 figure does not equal 14+13 or 14+12, so either a different cut or a reporting error is involved.

### Gaps
- No TBR, TAR, mean glucose, GMI, insulin dose, or hypoglycaemia event counts have been disclosed for the adult (73.8%) cohort anywhere.
- The ADA 2026 late-breaking abstract text (2883-LB) and the ATTD 2026 abstract text could not be retrieved (diabetesjournals.org is behind a bot-check; liebertpub.com returns 403); the only numbers are second-hand.
- No per-age-group split, no T2D feasibility data, no 90-day pivotal data of any kind exist publicly.

## Key Question 7 — Critiques and caveats from independent clinicians, analysts and advocates

### Takeaway

### Inferences
- A 90-day study phase ending ~Jan 2027 plus "lots of analytics" implies a PMA-supplement/510(k) filing around Q1–Q2 CY2027; first pivotal data are therefore unlikely before ATTD 2027 (Feb/Mar) and more likely ADA 2027 (June), consistent with management's refusal to commit.
- EU path: NEXUS (vs 780G, non-inferiority) is sized and timed as the CE-mark dataset; ELEVATE reads out well after the US launch target, so EU launch likely trails the US.
