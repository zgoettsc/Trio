## Key Question 1: Tandem Control-IQ / Control-IQ+ (t:slim X2 and Mobi)

### Takeaway
Control-IQ is a 30-minute-ahead, prediction-based hybrid closed loop built on the user's own basal/CF/CR profile with fixed (non-customizable) targets: basal is cut below a predicted 112.5 mg/dL, suspended below 70, raised above 160, and a 60%-attenuated automatic correction (to 110 mg/dL target) fires at most hourly when glucose is predicted >180 mg/dL. Control-IQ+ (2025) kept the same control rules but widened TDI/weight ranges (5-200 U/day, 9-200 kg), allowed temp basals and 8-hour extended boluses in automation, and gained the first FDA type 2 indication for Tandem (Feb 2025; NEJM 2IQP: TIR 48%→64%, HbA1c −0.6 pp vs control). A fully closed-loop "Tandem Freedom" (no meal announcement; UVA AIDANET lineage) has 72-hour feasibility data (median TIR 61%, overnight 96%) and a pivotal trial was planned to start in 2026 with FDA filing targeted for 2027.

### Cited Findings

**Control strategy, targets and dosing rules (cleared labeling)**
- FDA De Novo DEN190034 (granted Dec 2019): "Control-IQ technology predicts glucose levels 30 minutes in the future based on prior iCGM readings, insulin delivery history, and user input (e.g., carbohydrate intake, exercise, and sleep schedule)... can be used to adjust or suspend basal insulin delivery every 5 minutes and automatically deliver correction boluses of insulin based on actual and predicted CGM sensor readings. Users must manually deliver meal boluses." — [FDA DEN190034 decision summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/DEN190034.pdf)
- "Control-IQ technology works to control glucose towards a glucose target range of 112.5-160 mg/dL during normal use. Glucose targets are not customizable but can be changed by a user if sleep or exercise modes are set or announced. During sleep mode, this range is changed to 112.5-120 mg/dL, and it is changed to 140-160 mg/dL during exercise mode." — [FDA DEN190034](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/DEN190034.pdf)
- "Using Control-IQ technology requires that users input their weight and their total daily insulin requirement"; original label: not for use with TDI <10 U/day or weight <55 lb, and not under age 6 (original indication ≥14 y). — [FDA DEN190034](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/DEN190034.pdf)
- Basal modulation thresholds (predicted glucose, 30 min ahead): above 160 mg/dL increase basal; 112.5-160 maintain user's programmed basal; below 112.5 decrease; fully suspend below 70 mg/dL. Auto-correction: "If glucose values are predicted to be above 180 mg/dL, Control-IQ technology calculates a correction bolus with a target of 110 mg/dL and delivers 60% of that value. It will do this up to once per hour as needed." — [Tandem provider page (search snippet)](https://tdcqawww.apos.tandemdiabetes.com/providers/products/control-iq); same rules restated by Tandem's Polin at ADA 2024 — [EMJ symposium summary, Aug 2024](https://www.emjreviews.com/flagship-journal/symposium/what-happens-when-people-dont-bolus-for-extended-periods-of-time-while-using-control-iq-technology/)
- Sleep activity: algorithm "is more aggressive at night – the goal is to arrive at 112.5-120 mg/dL by the time the user wakes up"; no auto-corrections during Sleep (correction boluses are not delivered in sleep mode per Tandem; this detail comes from secondary coverage) — [diaTribe (search snippet)](https://diatribe.org/tandems-control-iq-cleared-united-states)
- Settings used: the algorithm modulates the user's programmed basal profile; bolus calculator uses the user's CF and CR; CGM value auto-populates the bolus calculator when Control-IQ is active. — [FDA DEN190034](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/DEN190034.pdf)
- Predicate/comparison table in iLet 510(k): Control-IQ (K200467) "Maximum Basal Rate 15 units/hr"; "Target BG is fixed to 110 mg/dL" when Control-IQ on; max bolus 25 U; "Maximum Automatic Bolus Size 6 units". — [FDA K220916 (iLet) summary, predicate comparison table](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K220916.pdf)

**Control-IQ+ (2025) changes**
- Control-IQ+ parameter expansion vs original: daily insulin 5-200 U/day (vs 10-100), body weight 9-200 kg (vs 25-140 kg), extended boluses up to 8 h with automation active, temporary basal rates without disabling automation. (Reported in the context of the 2026 CE mark but these are the same Control-IQ+ features as the US release.) — [Diabetotech, ADA 2026 closed-loop update, 18 Jun 2026](https://www.diabetotech.com/blog/closed-loop-updates-from-ada-2026)
- Label warning for Control-IQ+: "should not be used in patients who require less than 5 units of insulin per day or who weigh less than 20 pounds (9 kilograms)". Previously extended boluses in automation were capped at 2 h; temp basals were only available with Control-IQ off. — [Business Wire / Tandem press release, 25 Feb 2025 (search snippet)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250225281388/en); [Close Concerns summary (search snippet)](https://cckb.closeconcerns.com/r/451ecb65)
- Real-world Control-IQ+ data (>40,000 users): TIR 73% at <10 U/day, 72% at 10-100 U/day, 61% at >100 U/day; most users rarely used temp basal or extended bolus. — [Diabetotech ADA 2026](https://www.diabetotech.com/blog/closed-loop-updates-from-ada-2026)
- Control-IQ+ runs on both t:slim X2 and Tandem Mobi; Mobi expected in selected European markets H2 2026. — [Diabetotech ADA 2026](https://www.diabetotech.com/blog/closed-loop-updates-from-ada-2026)

**Clinical outcomes, T1D (peer-reviewed)**
- Pivotal iDCL trial (Brown et al., NEJM 2019;381:1707-1717; n=168, 2:1, ages 14-71, 6 months): TIR 61±17% → 71±12% (closed loop) vs 59±14% unchanged (SAP); adjusted difference +11 pp (95% CI 9-14); time <70 mg/dL difference −0.88 pp (95% CI −1.19 to −0.57); HbA1c difference −0.33 pp (95% CI −0.53 to −0.13); median 90% time in closed loop; no severe hypoglycemia; 1 DKA (closed loop). — [Europe PMC record, PMID 31618560](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/31618560)
- FDA tables from the same trial: post-randomization TIR 71±12% vs 59±14%; % <70 mg/dL 1.58% vs 2.25%; % <54 0.29% vs 0.35%; HbA1c 7.06 vs 7.39; mean glucose 156 vs 170 mg/dL; nighttime mean glucose 150 vs 170; nighttime TAR >180 22.5% vs 39.1%. — [FDA DEN190034](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/DEN190034.pdf)
- Real-world 1-year (Breton & Kovatchev, DT&T 2021): 9,451 users (83% T1D), median TIR 63.6% → 73.6% over 12 months; time <70 ~1%; median 94.2% time in automation. — [PMC8501470](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8501470/)
- Real-world "no bolus" behavior (Tandem, ADA 2024 poster; 291,769 users, Jan 2020-Dec 2023): 28,790 users (~1 in 10) had ≥7-day gaps with ≤1 manual bolus/day; on no-bolus days median TIR 62% (IQR 50-74%) vs 57-60% on bolus days in the same users; TBR <70 under 0.6% in both. — [EMJ, 23 Aug 2024](https://www.emjreviews.com/flagship-journal/symposium/what-happens-when-people-dont-bolus-for-extended-periods-of-time-while-using-control-iq-technology/)
- Meta-analysis of 7 RCTs of Control-IQ in T1D (daytime vs nighttime control) published 2025. — [PMC12341251](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12341251/)

**Clinical outcomes, T2D (peer-reviewed)**
- 2IQP trial (Kudva et al., NEJM 2025;392(18):1801-1812, online 19 Mar 2025): 319 adults with insulin-treated T2D, 2:1 AID vs continued therapy + CGM, 13 weeks. HbA1c 8.2→7.3% (AID) vs 8.1→7.7% (control); adjusted difference −0.6 pp (95% CI −0.8 to −0.4). TIR 48±24% → 64±16% vs 51±21% → 52±21%; difference 14 pp (95% CI 11-17). Hypoglycemia low in both groups; one severe hypoglycemia event in AID group. **Fully closed loop / "no meal announcement" (announced; feasibility peer-reviewed)**

### Inferences
- Control-IQ's control law is best described as a prediction-based rule set (predicted-glucose thresholds acting on the user's basal profile) with an attenuated correction-bolus layer; the FDA summary never calls it MPC, and the original UVA "USS Virginia" lineage is often described as an MPC-derived/"safety supervision module" design. Treat "MPC" labels in secondary press as loose.
- Because the controller only modulates the user's programmed basal and uses user CF/CR, its ceiling is bounded by setting quality; this explains Tandem's observed RWD gradient (TIR 61% in >100 U/day users) and why Control-IQ+ widened ranges rather than changing dosing logic.
- The no-bolus RWD (TIR ~62% without boluses in a self-selected subgroup, TBR <0.6%) is the practical "no-input" performance floor of the current hybrid algorithm; the Freedom FCL feasibility (61% median TIR in a deliberately adversarial high-fat/high-carb 72-h protocol) suggests meal detection mostly buys overnight/post-meal recovery rather than daytime peaks.

### Gaps
- Could not read the Control-IQ user guide or diaTribe page directly (403); the 60%/once-per-hour/180 rule is confirmed by Tandem's provider page and a Tandem employee's ADA 2024 summary, but exact rules for auto-correction suppression (e.g., within sleep mode, after a manual bolus, max auto-bolus 6 U) rest on the K220916 predicate table and secondary sources.
- No published technical description of the Freedom FCL algorithm (meal-detection method, dosing fraction) was found; the only data are the 10-patient JDST feasibility study.
- No confirmation (as of Oct 2026) that the Tandem FCL pivotal trial has actually begun; latest explicit statement was May 2026 ("planned to start this year").
- TBR values from the 2IQP T2D trial were not in the abstract retrieved; the NEJM full text was not accessed.

## Key Question 2: Insulet Omnipod 5 (SmartAdjust) and next-generation plans

### Takeaway
Omnipod 5 runs an on-pod, 60-minute-horizon predictive controller (Insulet's lineage papers call it personalized MPC; the Dec-2025 FDA summary calls it "rule-based and predictive control logic") that delivers microboluses every 5 minutes toward a user-selectable target (100-150 mg/dL since Dec 2025; previously 110-150) and derives all automated dosing from an "Adaptive Rate" equal to ~50% of a decaying-weighted TDI recomputed at each pod change, with automated delivery capped at 4x the Adaptive Rate (12 micro-corrections/hour). It is cleared for T1D ≥2 y and T2D ≥18 y (Aug 2024; SECURE-T2D HbA1c −0.8 pp, TIR 45→66%). Pivotal T1D TIR was 73.9% (adults/adolescents) and 68.0% (children); real-world TIR ~68% (n≈70,000). Next-gen: an "enhanced" Omnipod 5 algorithm (100 mg/dL target; stay-in-automation alarm change) rolled out in the US June 2026; Omnipod 6 (adaptive learning algorithm, up to 50% more automated insulin, STRIVE: TIR 70→73%) filing 2026/launch 2027; a T2D fully closed-loop pod (no meal entry) targeted 2028.

### Cited Findings

**Control strategy and architecture (cleared labeling)**
- FDA 510(k) K203774 (SmartAdjust, cleared Jan 2022): "uses data from a connected iCGM along with user-defined parameters to predict future glucose trends and automatically increase, decrease, or suspend the delivery of insulin... The Omnipod 5 iAGC software resides on the Omnipod 5 ACE Pump (the Omnipod 5 Pod and Omnipod 5 App)." Three states: Automated Mode ("calculates insulin delivery every 5 minutes based on the user-customizable glucose target (110-150 mg/dL)"), Automated: Limited ("enabled when the Omnipod 5 iAGC is not receiving data from a connected iCGM for 20 minutes or more and during sensor warm-up... the user will receive no more than pre-programmed basal insulin"), and Activity ("the algorithm reduces insulin delivery and sets a temporary glucose target of 150 mg/dL"). — [FDA K203774 summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf20/K203774.pdf)
- Dec 2025 510(k) K251779 (decision 3 Dec 2025, predicate K241777): "calculates insulin micro-boluses every 5 minutes, based on predicted glucose over a 60-minute horizon. It can also give autocorrection boluses for hyperglycemia"; control described as "rule-based and predictive control logic"; Limited Mode caps basal at the lower of the preprogrammed rate or a rate based on past usage; target now 100-150 mg/dL; Automated Delivery Restriction alert now triggers 2 h (was 1 h) after reaching a constraint bound and users remain in Automated Mode after acknowledging; "no changes to the algorithm's design, architecture, logic, or principles of operation." Evidence: in-silico trial of 125 digital twins x 72 scenarios at 100 mg/dL (TIR 65.9%, TBR<70 1.8%, TDI 49.4 U) plus RWD extrapolation from 32,332 users (predicted TIR 66.3% at 100 mg/dL). — [Innolitics OCR of FDA K251779 summary](https://fda.innolitics.com/device/K251779)
- Insulet's lineage papers describe a "Personalized Model Predictive Control Algorithm" (Buckingham/Forlenza, DT&T 2018-2019; DOI 10.1089/dia.2018.0138, 10.1089/dia.2019.0017), and an Insulet training document names SmartAdjust an MPC algorithm. — [Omnipod research page (search snippet)](https://www.omnipod.com/hcp/clinical-resources/research); [Insulet training doc (search snippet)](https://moodle.umontpellier.fr/mod/resource/view.php?id=1060001)

**Adaptive basal (TDI-based) and limits (company HCP documentation)**
- "The algorithm assumes about 50% of total daily insulin (TDI) is basal. This becomes the Adaptive Rate"; "recalculated with each Pod change, using actual insulin delivered"; first pod: TDI estimated from programmed basal rates, "the first Pod is more conservative, and maximum delivery is restricted"; after first pod change (and 48 h of wear) TDI comes from insulin history and "Automated maximum delivery is no longer restricted and can reach up to 4x the Adaptive Rate"; corrections "up to 12 times per hour". Example: 50 U/day TDI → Adaptive Rate ~25 U/day; if boluses fall to 10 U/day, TDI→~40, Adaptive Rate→~20 U/day. Changes to basal rates or Max Basal after startup have no effect on the algorithm. Suggested bolus settings: ICR ≤350/TDI; CF ≤1500/TDI. Not for use <2 y or <5 U/day. — [Omnipod 5 algorithm (HCP page)](https://www.omnipod.com/hcp/clinical-resources/provider-resources/omnipod-5-algorithm)
- TDI uses "a decaying weighted average of TDI and weights the most recent 4 to 5 Pods most heavily"; Target Glucose "is the only setting that directly affects algorithm-driven delivery" (10 mg/dL steps); "Use Sensor" in SmartBolus can add up to 30% to a suggested bolus; supported CGMs: Dexcom G7, G6 (discontinuing), Libre 2 Plus. — [Omnipod HCP: switching from other AID](https://omnipod.com/hcp/blog/tips-guidelines/switching-from-AID-to-Omnipod5)
- Adaptation "generally stabilizes after 2-3 pod changes"; "doesn't learn time-of-day dietary routines"; basal can't go below 0.05 U/h; users should still bolus for carbs and corrections. — [Omnipod 5 algorithm FAQ](https://www.omnipod.com/current-podders/resources/omnipod-5/faqs/smartadjust-technology)
- Activity feature: Automated Mode only, 1-24 h in 1-h steps, target 150 mg/dL and reduced delivery; a correction bolus during Activity uses the programmed target/Correct Above, not 150. — [Omnipod HCP activity tips](https://omnipod.com/hcp/blog/tips-guidelines/activity-feature-tips); 2025 Diabetes Care RCT: enabling Activity 30 or 60 min before prolonged exercise reduced insulin and attenuated glucose drops vs Auto, but hypoglycemia incidence did not differ. — [PMC12368368 (search snippet)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12368368)

**Unannounced meals / user input**
- Users must enter carbs for SmartBolus (ICR/CF/DIA settings), but automated microboluses respond to rising glucose; Insulet-reported hybrid vs fully-closed definitions: hybrid requires carb counting; "fully closed-loop" adapts without user intervention or clinician-set initial settings. — [MedTech Dive, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/insulet-posts-clinical-data-on-fully-closed-loop-insulin-delivery-system/814516/)
- "Inconsistent bolusing can cause TDI and the Adaptive Rate to be underestimated" (i.e., missed boluses lower future automated capacity). — [Omnipod 5 algorithm (HCP page)](https://www.omnipod.com/hcp/clinical-resources/provider-resources/omnipod-5-algorithm)

Safety: 3 severe hypoglycemia (0.004/person-month), 1 DKA (infusion site). — [FDA K203774 summary tables](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf20/K203774.pdf); [Omnipod summary of Brown 2021](https://www.omnipod.com/hcp/blog/clinical-studies/brown-21-summary)

- FDA cleared SmartAdjust for adults ≥18 with T2D on 26 Aug 2024 (indication now T1D ≥2 y, T2D ≥18 y). — [Insulet investor release, 26 Aug 2024](https://investors.insulet.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnipod-5-Automated-Insulin-Delivery-System-is-now-FDA-cleared-for-People-with-Type-2-Diabetes/default.aspx)

- Insulet FCL data in T2D (reported 11-12 Mar 2026, ATTD): 24 adults, final algorithm with no bolus insulin: TIR 68% ("24% improvement over standard injection therapy"), median TBR 0.14%, no SH/DKA, >90% entered extension; timeline: pivotal 2026, 510(k) 2027, launch 2028. — [MedTech Dive, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/insulet-posts-clinical-data-on-fully-closed-loop-insulin-delivery-system/814516/)
- Insulet lowered FY2026 revenue guidance in Aug 2026 citing T2D scaling learnings. — [Benzinga, Aug 2026 (search snippet)](https://www.benzinga.com/trading-ideas/movers/26/08/60945127/omnipod-sales-soar-but-insulets-outlook-cut-sparks-stock-selloff)
- Abbott's dual glucose-ketone sensor (Libre Duo): Abbott has announced AID integrations with Sequel, Tandem, Beta Bionics, Ypsomed and CamDiab; a 2025 industry table listed Omnipod 5 as "No announcement yet". — [Abbott newsroom (search snippet)](https://www.abbott.com/en-us/corpnewsroom/strategy-and-strength/abbotts-biowearable-one-sensor-for-glucose-ketones); [Drug Delivery Business (search snippet)](https://www.drugdeliverybusiness.com/?p=67436)

### Inferences
- The "max basal from TDI" limit is explicit: automated delivery ≤4x Adaptive Rate, where Adaptive Rate ≈ 0.5 x TDI(decaying average over ~4-5 pods). Users who under-bolus therefore shrink their own automation ceiling, which is the specific failure mode Omnipod 6's "learns insulin requirements... performs better for people who bolus less" and "up to 50% more automated insulin" are designed to fix.
- STRIVE's +3 pp TIR with a +0.3 pp TBR rise suggests Omnipod 6 is a more aggressive version of the same TDI-anchored design rather than a new control paradigm; the true "no-input" product is the separate 2028 T2D FCL pod (feasibility TIR 68%, TBR 0.14% in T2D).
- The Dec-2025 "enhanced" clearance was justified entirely by simulation plus RWD extrapolation (no new clinical study), which indicates FDA accepts digital-twin evidence for target-range changes in an already-cleared iAGC.

### Gaps
- Insulet does not publish the MPC cost function, model, or per-cycle microbolus sizing; the "60-min horizon, every 5 min, ≤12 corrections/h, ≤4x Adaptive Rate" are the only public constraints.
- No peer-reviewed STRIVE publication found as of Oct 2026 (only ADA abstract 1068-OR via secondary coverage); SmartAdjust 2.0 vs "Omnipod 6" naming appears to refer to the same program but this was not confirmed by Insulet text.
- No source found for Omnipod 5 handling of unannounced meals beyond generic "responds to rising glucose with microboluses"; no published meal-detection logic.

## Key Question 3: Beta Bionics iLet Bionic Pancreas (and 3D Intelligence, Mint, bihormonal, T2D)

### Takeaway
The iLet is initialized with body weight only and runs three parallel adaptive controllers (basal, correction/"bolus", meal) that the user/clinician cannot tune; meals are announced qualitatively (Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner x Usual/More/Less, with "Less" ≈50% and "More" ≈150% of usual) and the meal controller learns dose size per meal type. Only three CGM targets exist (110/120/130 mg/dL; "usual" = 120). Pivotal RCT (NEJM 2022, n=326, 13 wk): HbA1c −0.5 pp vs standard care, TIR +11 pp (adults) / +10 pp (children), with no significant change in time <54 and numerically more severe hypoglycemia (17.7 vs 10.8 events/100 pt-yr, P=0.39). Beta Bionics reports >40,000 users; real-world GMI ≈7.3% from HbA1c 8.9%; ~16% of users announce <1 meal/day and achieve ~62% TIR. As of Sept 2026: Mint patch pump cleared (T1D ≥6 y), the "3D Intelligence" next-gen algorithm (Original/Optimized/Conservative modes) is under 510(k) review, a T2D pivotal started July 2026 (clearance targeted mid-2027), and a fully-closed-loop pivotal is planned; bihormonal remains pre-pivotal.

### Cited Findings

**Architecture, inputs, limits (cleared labeling)**
- FDA K220916 (cleared May 2023): "The iLet Dosing Decision Software only requires initialization with the user's body mass (body weight)... does not require carbohydrate counting... does not require any information about the user's total daily dose of insulin, basal or long-acting insulin requirements, or insulin correction factors. It is an insulin titration system that requires no insulin-dose determinations by the user or provider." It "has three insulin controllers (algorithms) running in parallel: an adaptive basal insulin controller, which continually adapts to each individual's basal metabolic need for insulin, an adaptive bolus controller which provides doses that are required above and beyond the basal metabolic needs, and an adaptive meal dose controller which provides insulin in response to a meal announcement." Responds every 5 minutes. BG-run mode continues basal "based on past requirements" and accepts fingerstick BG as iCGM data when CGM is lost. Indication: T1D ≥6 y. — [FDA K220916 summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K220916.pdf)
- Predicate comparison table (iLet vs Control-IQ): iLet effective basal 0-11.5 U/h (internal, cannot be set); CGM targets 110, 120, 130 mg/dL; max bolus 24 U for a meal announcement, 30 U overall; max automatic bolus 3 U in response to CGM glucose (6 U in response to an isolated BG when CGM offline). — [FDA K220916 summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K220916.pdf)
- User guide: basal algorithm "calculates the basal dose needed based on your glucose profile from the previous 24 hours"; correction algorithm uses "glucose profile from the previous 24 hours, current CGM glucose and trend, and insulin on board"; meal algorithm "will learn and adapt as you announce meals over time." Users cannot "program any insulin settings (no basal rates, correction factors, or carb ratios)", "give a correction dose of insulin", "enter carbohydrates", or "change how the iLet is automating insulin delivery." — [Beta Bionics Educational Resource Guide LA000061](https://www.betabionics.com/wp-content/uploads/LA000061_G-Educational-Resource-Guide.pdf)
- Meal sizing: Usual = typical carbs for that meal type; More ≈ 1.5x usual; Less ≈ 0.5x usual; do not announce if <25% of usual; announce within 30 min of starting to eat ("Announcing more than 30 minutes after you have started eating can result in severe hypoglycemia"); the "Less" option is unavailable until the iLet has learned the Usual dose for that meal type; never use meal announcements to correct highs. — [Educational Resource Guide](https://www.betabionics.com/wp-content/uploads/LA000061_G-Educational-Resource-Guide.pdf)
- Meal dosing behavior (peer-reviewed description): "The system delivers about 75% of its estimated insulin need immediately, then adds or withholds postprandial basal or correction insulin as needed." — [Lynch et al., DT&T 2022 extension study (PMC9529297)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9529297)
- Hypoglycemia handling / exercise: there is no exercise mode; options are to disconnect (up to 1 h before exercise) or stay connected ("The iLet will continue to increase or decrease insulin dosing in response to your CGM glucose levels"); users are warned glucose "could rise above 250 mg/dL for a few hours" after meals and "could drop below 70 mg/dL or 54 mg/dL, especially during or soon after exercise." BG-run mode lasts ≤48 h (first 7 days of use) or ≤72 h, requests a BG every 4 h, shuts off basal for 1 h on a low BG entry, and stops all insulin when it expires. — [Educational Resource Guide](https://www.betabionics.com/wp-content/uploads/LA000061_G-Educational-Resource-Guide.pdf)

**Clinical outcomes (peer-reviewed)**
- Pivotal RCT (Bionic Pancreas Research Group, NEJM 2022;387:1161-1172; online Sept 2022): 326 participants aged 6-79 with T1D, 2:1, 13 weeks (219 bionic pancreas with aspart/lispro, 107 standard care). HbA1c 7.9→7.3% vs 7.7→7.7%; adjusted difference −0.5 pp (95% CI −0.6 to −0.3; P<0.001). Time <54 mg/dL adjusted difference 0.0 pp (95% CI −0.1 to 0.04), noninferior. Severe hypoglycemia 17.7 vs 10.8 events/100 participant-years (P=0.39); no DKA. — [Europe PMC record, PMID 36170500](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/36170500)
- Same trial, ADA 2022 presentation: TIR +11 pp (2.6 h/day) adults, +10 pp (2.4 h/day) children; adult SH 7 events (25.5/100 pt-yr) vs 2 (14.2); pediatric 3 (10.4) vs 1 (7.3); infusion-set failures caused 30 hyperglycemia AEs per group. — [Healio, 7 Jun 2022](https://www.healio.com/news/endocrinology/20220607/bionic-pancreas-reduces-hba1c-improves-time-in-range-in-type-1-diabetes-pivotal-data)
- Extension study (Lynch et al., DT&T 2022; 90 participants from the SC arm, 13 weeks): HbA1c 7.7→7.1% (−0.55); TIR 53→65% (+12.0 pp); <70 2.53→2.08%; <54 0.56→0.49% (ns); >180 44→33%; mean glucose 182→164; total daily insulin 0.82→0.85 U/kg (ns); 2 SH events in one adult; 1 pediatric DKA (infusion set). — [PMC9529297](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9529297)
- Systematic review/meta-analysis (2025; 4-5 RCTs, ~1,130 patients): HbA1c SMD −0.50 (95% CI −0.63 to −0.38); TIR SMD +0.58; TBR<70 and <54 not different; TAR>180 SMD −0.52; severe hypoglycemia OR 2.22 (95% CI 0.83-5.94, ns); any adverse event OR 15.48 (mostly site/skin/GI); authors note trials were 13 weeks and TDI was not reported. — [PMC12568523](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12568523/)
- FDA K220916 clinical summary: two DKA events in the iLet group related to infusion set failures; BG-run ancillary study (48-60 h) showed safe use with meter input. — [FDA K220916 summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K220916.pdf)

**Real-world data (company-reported)**
- Beta Bionics 8-K (14 Sep 2026): RWD May 2023-May 2025, n=14,952 with baseline HbA1c: >84% met ADA hypoglycemia goals (<4% <70, <1% <54); mean HbA1c 8.9% → GMI 7.3% (−1.5); GMI 7.4% by day 2 (n=30,077 sessions); "fully-closed loop users" (~16%, n=2,416, announcing <1 meal/day): ~62% TIR, ~83% met hypo goals, HbA1c 9.4% → GMI 7.5%; 530 users announcing no meals: 9.4% → 7.4%; post-market SH 8.1 users per 100 patient-years (n=2,741, ~434 pt-yr) vs 9.6 cited for Control-IQ (cross-study, not controlled). — [Beta Bionics Form 8-K, 14-15 Sep 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001674632/000119312526392088/d153052d8k.htm)
- ADA 2026 posters: >16,000 commercial users, HbA1c-to-GMI reduction 1.5% (T1D) and 1.8% (T2D, off-label); >30,000 starts, median time to long-term TIR 1.5 days; public RWD dashboard launched June 2026. — [Diabetotech ADA 2026](https://www.diabetotech.com/blog/closed-loop-updates-from-ada-2026)
- Two-year RWD page: HbA1c→GMI improvement 1.6%; median time <54 mg/dL 0.3%. — [Beta Bionics RWE page](https://www.betabionics.com/ilet-bionic-pancreas-real-world-evidence/)
- Engagement analysis (ENDO 2025 abstract MON-613): best outcomes in users announcing 2.5 to <4.5 meals/day; ≥4.5 announcements/day may reflect "ghost" meals used to obtain correction insulin, risking stacking/hypoglycemia. — [JES 2025 abstract MON-613](https://academic.oup.com/jes/article/9/Supplement_1/bvaf149.974/8297406)

**Pipeline (announced)**
- Mint patch pump FDA-cleared 14 Sep 2026 (T1D ≥6 y): two-piece reusable/disposable, 200 U, 3-day wear + 12 h grace, Dexcom G7/G7 15 Day/Libre 3 Plus, iOS/Android control; full US launch Q1 2027 via pharmacy. — [Beta Bionics 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001674632/000119312526392088/d153052d8k.htm); [HCPLive, 14 Sep 2026](https://www.hcplive.com/view/fda-clears-beta-bionics-mint-insulin-patch-pump-for-diabetes)
- "3D Intelligence" next-gen dosing algorithm submitted via 510(k) (announced 14 Sep 2026; not yet cleared): three selectable experiences — Original (current iLet algorithm, >40,000 users), Optimized (incorporates RWD learnings), Conservative ("more measured response when correcting high glucose"); to power iLet 3D (expected end-2026) and Mint 3D (Q1 2027), subject to clearance. — [Beta Bionics 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001674632/000119312526392088/d153052d8k.htm)
- T2D: pivotal trial of iLet in adults with T2D began enrollment July 2026; indication expansion expected ~mid-2027 subject to FDA. A fully-closed-loop algorithm is in development with a pivotal in T1D and T2D "in the coming quarters." — [Beta Bionics 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001674632/000119312526392088/d153052d8k.htm); [MarketBeat Q2 2026 call highlights](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/beta-bionics-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-07-29/)
- Bihormonal (insulin + glucagon): not commercial; requires separate pump and algorithm 510(k)s plus an NDA for the glucagon product; completed a Phase 2a feasibility in New Zealand in Q4 2025 with another Phase 2a in H1 2026 then Phase 2b; glucagon partner Xeris. — [Beta Bionics 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001674632/000119312526392088/d153052d8k.htm); [OCBJ (search snippet)](https://www.ocbj.com/oc-homepage/beta-bionics-2-year-revenue-jumps-736-to-103-2-million/); [Beta Bionics S-1, Jan 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1674632/000119312525002177/d880651ds1.htm)
- Beta Bionics is remediating an FDA warning letter on complaint handling/post-market surveillance (Q2 2026 call). — [MarketBeat Q2 2026 call highlights](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/beta-bionics-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-07-29/)

### Inferences
- The iLet is the only cleared US system with no clinician-tunable insulin parameters; its "learning" is per-meal-type dose adaptation plus 24-h-profile-driven basal/correction adaptation, not a TDI anchor. Its limitations in the RCT (no reduction in TBR, numerically higher SH, higher AE rate) are consistent with the design's willingness to dose corrections aggressively; the "Conservative" 3D Intelligence mode is presumably a response to this.
- The published RCT HbA1c baseline (7.9%) is lower than the RWD baseline (8.9%), which explains why company GMI deltas (−1.5) exceed trial deltas (−0.5); do not compare them directly.
- The company's "fully closed-loop users" (no meal announcements, ~62% TIR) is the best available estimate of true no-input performance of a cleared US system, though it is a self-selected subgroup.

### Gaps
- No public description of the correction controller's control law (press and academic sources have called it MPC-like; the FDA summary only says "adaptive bolus controller"). The 3-U max automatic correction bolus and 24-U meal cap are the only numeric constraints found.
- The NEJM abstract does not report absolute TIR per arm; secondary sources give +11/+10 pp and the ~65% figure is from the extension/educational material rather than the NEJM abstract retrieved.
- No peer-reviewed real-world iLet paper with TIR/TBR distributions was located; all RWD is company-reported.
- No published data yet on 3D Intelligence "Optimized" or "Conservative" modes.

## Key Question 4: Sequel MedTech twiist running Tidepool Loop (and roadmap: DBLG2, CGMs, Lilly)

### Takeaway
twiist runs the FDA-cleared Tidepool Loop (K203689, Jan 2023; ported to the pump as "DEKA Loop", K234055), i.e., the DIY Loop MPC-style 6-hour predictive algorithm (insulin + carb + retrospective-correction + momentum effects, dynamic carb absorption) with temp-basal dosing every 5 min; unlike DIY Loop's optional "automatic bolus" strategy, the cleared version only recommends correction boluses that the user must confirm. Users enter basal, ISF, CR, DIA-fixed model, max basal/bolus, correction range (87-180 mg/dL), glucose safety limit (67-110), and pre-meal/workout presets. Evidence is mostly the Loop Observational Study (DIY Loop on other pumps; n=558, TIR 67→73%; intended-use subset n=175 TIR 62→70%, SH 192→42/100 pt-yr) plus twiist first-user RWD (n=274, TIR 77%, TBR 2.9%) and an ADA 2026 T2D pivotal (n=307, TIR 57→73%, HbA1c 8.1→7.4%). Roadmap: Libre 3 Plus and Eversense 365 live; Abbott dual glucose-ketone sensor planned; Diabeloop DBLG2 (FDA-cleared Dec 2025; weight+TDD initialization, unannounced-meal handling) to be offered on twiist by 2027. No current Lilly collaboration found.

### Cited Findings

**Regulatory / algorithm (cleared labeling)**
- Tidepool Loop 510(k) K203689 (SE decision 23 Jan 2023; predicate Control-IQ K200467): indicated for T1D ≥6 y; "predicts glucose up to 6 hours ahead using prior iCGM readings, insulin delivery history, and user-entered carbohydrates and exercise"; "adjusts or suspends basal delivery every 5 minutes"; Bolus Recommendation Tool recommends correction boluses and "the user must confirm before delivery"; user-set max basal and max bolus; Correction Range 87-180 mg/dL (time-of-day segments); Glucose Safety Limit 67-110 mg/dL; presets: normal, pre-meal, workout. — [Innolitics summary of FDA K203689](https://fda.innolitics.com/device/K203689)
- DEKA Loop (K234055) "was a ported version of the Tidepool Loop device cleared under K203689... intended to function identically to the Tidepool Loop, with the only differences being the physical location of the algorithm and the programming language." — [FDA K234055 review (search snippet)](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/K234055.pdf)
- twiist cleared by FDA in 2024; nationwide US availability announced 9 Mar 2026 ($0 first month then ≤$50/month pharmacy model); integrated with Libre 3 Plus and Eversense 365; "additional integrations planned for later this year including Abbott's future dual glucose-ketone sensor"; future support for Diabeloop DBLG2. — [GlobeNewswire, 9 Mar 2026 (search snippet; page returned 403)](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/09/3251786/0/en/sequel-brings-twiist-nationwide-redefining-automated-insulin-delivery-category-with-industry-first-innovation.html)

**Loop algorithm mechanics (open-source documentation; the cleared algorithm is a port)**
- Every 5 min, Loop predicts glucose over the insulin activity duration (6 h) using current/historical glucose, insulin, and entered carbs via "model predictive control (MPC) with the user's therapy settings"; glucose safety limit → temp basal 0 if any predicted value falls below it; correction range midpoint is the target. — [LoopDocs algorithm overview](https://loopkit.github.io/loopdocs/operation/algorithm/overview/)
- Four summed effects: insulin (exponential model, 6-h DIA, peak ~1 h), carbs (absorption starts 10 min after entry, dynamic absorption from "insulin counteraction effect" with floor = carbs/(1.5 x entered duration)), retrospective correction (30-min window; velocity decays to zero over 60 min; integral RC optional experiment), momentum (last 3 readings, fades over ~20 min). — [LoopDocs prediction](https://loopkit.github.io/loopdocs/operation/algorithm/prediction/)
- Dosing: dose = (eventual glucose − target)/ISF; temp basal rate = scheduled basal + 2 x dose (30-min temp), capped at max temp basal, floored at 0; re-evaluated each CGM reading, not reissued if a suitable temp has ≥10 min remaining; DIY "Automatic Bolus" strategy delivers 40% of the recommended correction (20-80% with glucose-based partial application experiment), whereas "Temp Basal Only" converts increases into temp basals; no automatic increase when glucose is below the correction range. — [LoopDocs automatic dosing](https://loopkit.github.io/loopdocs/operation/algorithm/auto-adjust/)
- Diabettech (15 Jan 2026): Tidepool Loop "lacks, by design, the unannounced-meal handling that DBLG2 offers" and "requires significant human involvement in setup and tuning" (citing PMID 33226840). — [Diabettech, 15 Jan 2026](https://www.diabettech.com/a-twiist-of-fate-why-sequel-is-moving-beyond-tidepool-to-embrace-diabeloop/)

### Inferences
- The cleared Tidepool Loop is functionally DIY Loop in "Temp Basal Only" mode with FDA-bounded settings (87-180 correction range; 67-110 safety limit) and no automatic boluses; this makes twiist the most setting-dependent and most user-input-dependent of the four systems (carb entry + manual confirmation of corrections), which is the opposite end of the spectrum from the iLet.
- The 77% TIR in twiist early adopters likely reflects a selected, experienced-looper cohort (consistent with the Loop Observational Study's high-SES population); TBR ~2.9% is the highest of the four systems' RWD figures, consistent with low targets (as low as 87 mg/dL) being allowed.
- Sequel adding DBLG2 signals that unannounced-meal handling and low-input setup are seen as necessary for mass-market adoption; the Diabettech framing of a "pivot" is editorial, not a Sequel statement.

### Gaps
- Could not read Sequel's twiist user guide; the exact treatment of "automatic correction boluses" (ADCES says No; FDA says recommend-and-confirm) and whether Integral Retrospective Correction or overrides beyond pre-meal/workout exist in twiist are unverified.
- No peer-reviewed twiist-specific outcomes paper as of Oct 2026 (ATTD 2026 poster; ADA 2026 T2D talk only).
- No Sequel public statement on an auto-bolus or fully-closed-loop version of Tidepool Loop; the FCL path on twiist appears to run through DBLG2.

## Key Question 5: Cross-system comparison — proximity to "no user input", TIR achieved, and tradeoffs

### Takeaway
Ranked by user input required: iLet (weight only; qualitative meal announcements; nothing tunable) < Omnipod 5 (basal profile to seed TDI, target, bolus settings; carb boluses expected) ≈ Control-IQ+ (full basal/CF/CR profile, weight, TDI; carb boluses expected; hourly 60% auto-corrections) < twiist/Tidepool Loop (full profile + carb entry + manual confirmation of corrections). Pivotal/RWD TIR, however, runs the other way for engaged users (twiist RWD 77%, Control-IQ 71-74%, Omnipod 5 68-74%, iLet ~65%), and the systems closest to "no input" show either higher TBR/SH (iLet) or lower TIR (~62% for no-bolus Control-IQ users and no-meal iLet users; 61% in Tandem Freedom 72-h FCL; 68% in Insulet T2D FCL). True no-announcement products (Tandem Freedom, Insulet T2D FCL pod, Beta Bionics FCL, DBLG2-on-twiist) are all announced for 2027-2028 and none is cleared as of Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Architecturally the four systems split into (a) profile-modulating controllers (Control-IQ, Tidepool Loop) whose performance tracks the quality of user basal/CF/CR, and (b) self-parameterizing controllers (Omnipod 5 via TDI; iLet via weight + 24-h adaptation). The industry trend across all four vendors' 2026-2028 roadmaps is toward (b) plus meal detection: Omnipod 6 "learns insulin requirements", Beta Bionics 3D Intelligence/FCL, Tandem Freedom with meal detection, DBLG2 on twiist.
- The observed ~61-68% TIR across every no-announcement dataset (Control-IQ no-bolus RWD, iLet no-meal RWD, Tandem Freedom, Insulet T2D FCL) versus 70-77% with announcements suggests roughly a 7-12 pp TIR cost of removing meal input with current insulin kinetics; vendors appear to accept this for T2D first (where TBR is very low: 0.14-0.4%) and plan T1D FCL after.
- Allowing low targets (twiist 87 mg/dL; Omnipod 100) raises TIR at a measurable TBR cost (twiist RWD 2.9%; Omnipod sim 1.8% at 100 vs 1.5% at 110), whereas Control-IQ's fixed 112.5-160 band and iLet's 110-130 options bound TBR at ~1% but cap TIR.

### Gaps
- No head-to-head RCT or network meta-analysis directly ranking Control-IQ, Omnipod 5, iLet and twiist was found; a 2025 NMA of AID RCTs in T1D exists (Stahl-Pehe et al., per search) but per-system results were not retrievable.
- Insulin-use (TDI) comparisons across systems are not reported consistently (iLet trials omit TDI; Omnipod reports insulin reduction in T2D; twiist T2D reports 92→72 U/day in basal-bolus users).
- Post-meal peak metrics (e.g., 2-h postprandial excursion) were not reported in any retrieved source for any system; TAR >180 is the only proxy available.
- The ADCES July 2026 comparison PDF, diaTribe system pages, and several PMC full texts returned HTTP 403, so some rule-level details are cited from search snippets rather than full page reads.
